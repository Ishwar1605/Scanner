  Reading a cell.
  LONG / SHORT — every condition agrees.
   ·  4/6 — evaluated, that many conditions passed on its stronger side; hover to see which failed, click to open it.
   ·  · — still not enough bricks after deepening to max history; that symbol is simply too quiet for that brick size.
  
Bricks column — how many bricks exist at each size, in column order: 0.75% / 1% / 1.5%.
  WMA21-of-RSI21 needs 44, so anything under that shows ·. The scanner deepens history on its own until it has them.
  
Brick size — the percentage is applied to the last price, rounded to that symbol's minimum tick,
  then snapped to the nearest 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 × 10ⁿ step. Verified against BTC 450, ETH 15, XRP 0.0075,
  1000PEPE 0.00003, DOGS 0.0000002 and TradingView's own AAPL example. TradingView uses the price
  at the moment the chart loaded, so if a symbol has moved a long way since you opened it, reload that
  chart to bring the two back in line.
  
Source — TradingView builds renko from the chart's own resolution, using either close or OHLC,
  and its default is Close. Set the dropdown to whatever your chart's Symbol tab says, or the bricks
  will not match. Brick boundaries sit on absolute multiples of the brick size.
  
Data: Delta Exchange India public API, fetched by this browser. Bricks are rebuilt from
  1m candles, with the standard two-brick reversal rule.
